Hello friends, our trainings will continue and the 
technology-independent development will continue. I am working on our 
PSD Operating System. I made a radical change about license choice. It 
was good to do this while we did not have a serious codebase yet. Now 
the system will be licensed with BSD-3-Clause. I chose the BSD-3-Clause 
license as the main license. This was already written in our code of 
conduct, but it was for institutions that were not within us who does 
not work directly with us. Now I have decided to apply the BSD license 
in general.

Thanks, happy hacking !
